Your safety comes first

{{BRAND}} is built so you can show you're available without putting yourself at risk.

Frequently asked questions

Why do you check my age?

Because {{BRAND}} is for adults only and profiles can contain adult content. Laws in France and a growing number of other countries require reliable age checks for this kind of service. An independent provider handles it. All it tells us is whether you're 18 or over, and we never see your ID.

Who can see my location?

Nobody sees your real location. It's rounded before it's saved, then your dot is placed at random inside a 500 m or 1 km square, depending on your setting. Distances are shown in ranges. You can appear only as part of an area count, hide your distance, drop off the map or delete your location at any time.

Is it free?

Yes, everything is free during launch. A {{PLUS}} plan will come later with extras like travel mode and advanced filters. You'll be told before any payment, and you can cancel in three clicks.

What happens when I report someone?

The moderation team gets your report with a secure copy of the latest messages, so you don't need screenshots. The other person isn't told it was you. If action is taken against an account, its owner gets a decision with the reasons and can appeal it, as the EU Digital Services Act requires.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Me and tap “Delete my account”. Your profile, photos, albums and messages are permanently deleted. You can download your data first. Reports about you are kept for a limited time, to keep members safe.

Why are there no outdoor cruising spots?

Sex in public is a criminal offence in most countries. In France, for example, it carries up to a year in prison and a €15,000 fine (article 222-32 of the Penal Code). And isolated parks and car parks are where ambushes happen. So {{BRAND}} shows verified venues: bars, clubs, saunas and parties.
